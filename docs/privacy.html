Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Oriona is an astrology app that calculates your birth chart and daily forecast entirely on your device. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Oriona has no user accounts, no login, and no server of its own. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data.

What you enter and where it stays

To build your chart, the app asks for your name, date, time and place of birth, and whether you would like the texts written for a woman or a man. This information is stored only in the app's local storage on your device, and is used only to calculate your chart and forecast. It is never transmitted to us. If you delete the app, it is gone.

City search

The app ships with an offline database of cities, so search works without a network. If your device is online, the app may additionally query the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service with the text you typed in the city field, in order to offer more precise results. Only that text is sent, and it is not linked to you. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription, the payment is processed by Apple. We never see your card details, and Apple only tells us that a valid subscription exists. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Oriona is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. If we ever begin collecting any data, we will say so here before it happens.
